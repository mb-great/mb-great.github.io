Questions people actually ask

  • How many 2 × 1 inch labels fit on A4?

    Four across and ten down, so forty per sheet, with a strip left over. If you want no waste the label has to stretch slightly — put 200 × 287 mm in above and the calculator shows you by how much.

  • How many round stickers fit on a sheet?

    Treat a circle as its bounding square. A 50 mm round sticker tiles like a 50 × 50 mm label. Hexagonal nesting fits more but almost no cutter supports it.

  • What does perfect tiling mean?

    The labels fill the printable area with no leftover strip. Getting there usually means the label size moves a little. The deviation columns show exactly how much — positive means slightly bigger than you asked for, negative means smaller.

  • Why does it suggest a size I did not ask for?

    Because your requested size does not divide evenly into the sheet. Nothing does, usually. The ranking shows you the closest options so you can pick which compromise you would rather make — a wider label or a shorter one.

  • Can I mix millimetres and inches?

    Yes. Sheet and label each have their own unit and they do not have to match.

  • Does it account for bleed and gutters?

    Not yet. Subtract your bleed from the printable area before entering it. If you need a 2 mm gap between labels, add it to the label size instead.
