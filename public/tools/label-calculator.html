How it works. The calculator tries every row and column count that fits your sheet, then ranks the results by how far the label size has to move from what you asked for. Rank #1 wastes the least. The deviation columns are the honest bit - a positive number means the label gets slightly bigger, negative means slightly smaller.

Common sheet sizes: A4 is 210 x 297 mm, Letter is 8.5 x 11 inches, and most home printers cannot print to the edge - measure your printable area, not the paper.
