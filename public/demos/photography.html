How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. Check the date

    Send the date and the venue. We reply within two days either way.

  2. Meet, properly

    A call or a coffee. If we are not right for your wedding we will say so and point you at someone who is.

  3. The day

    We arrive early, find the light, and stay quiet. Two of us, so nothing gets missed.

  4. Your gallery

    Six weeks, around 600 edited photographs, downloadable at full size. Yours to print anywhere.

"The best photograph from most weddings is one nobody noticed being taken."

We shoot about twenty weddings a year on purpose. It keeps the editing honest and it means we are never running two galleries behind.

We travel. Anywhere in the country is included, further afield is just the cost of getting there.
