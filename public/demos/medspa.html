The studio

One room, one practitioner.

Most places run four rooms and a rotating team. We run one. It means fewer appointments in a day and it means the person who assessed your face is the person holding the needle.

First visits are 45 minutes and half of that is talking. If we don't think a treatment will do what you're hoping, we say so before you've paid for it.

"We'd rather turn something down than do it badly and have you living with it for nine months."
