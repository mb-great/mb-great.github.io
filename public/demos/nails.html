Policies

The fine print, kept short.

We are a two-person studio, so one empty chair is half the day. These keep prices fair for everyone.

  1. $20 deposit to bookComes off your total. Full art sets need $30.
  2. 24-hour cancellationsReschedule or cancel up to 24 hours ahead and the deposit moves with you. Inside 24 hours it is kept.
  3. Running late?Text us. Up to 10 minutes is fine. After 15 we may need to shorten the service or rebook.
  4. 7-day fix guaranteeA lift or chip in the first week is repaired free.
  5. Just you, pleaseThe studio is small, so no extra guests unless they are booked too.
