Small group · Coached · Capped at 12

Twelve people.
One coach.
No mirrors.

Strength and conditioning in small groups, programmed in six-week blocks. Everyone gets their numbers written down and reviewed. If you have never touched a barbell we will start you at the bar and that is completely normal here.

See pricing
Classes

Four things, done properly

Strength

60 min

Squat, press, hinge, pull. Linear progression for the first twelve weeks, then blocks. You will keep a log and we will read it.

Conditioning

45 min

Intervals on bikes, rowers and sleds. Hard, short, and scaled to what you actually did last week rather than a leaderboard.

Barbell club

75 min

Snatch and clean and jerk. Technical, slow, and frankly frustrating for the first two months. Worth it after that.

Open gym

Anytime

Full floor access with a coach present. Follow your own programme or ours. No booking needed.

Intro session

30 min · Free

One-to-one. We look at how you move, talk about what you want, and tell you honestly whether this is the right room for it.

What we don't do

—

No classes over twelve people. No unqualified coaches on the floor. No contracts longer than a month.
