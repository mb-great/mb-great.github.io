The practice

Four surgeries, one waiting room, no upselling.

Nobody here works on commission, which is the shortest way to explain why we will not sell you veneers you did not ask about. If a tooth can be watched rather than drilled, we will say so and note it for next time.

We are open late on Tuesdays and Thursdays and we keep two slots free every morning for emergencies. If you are in pain, ring first thing rather than waiting.

Questions

The ones we get asked most.

  • Are you taking new patients?

    Yes, for private treatment. There is no waiting list at the moment.

  • I have not been to a dentist in years.

    That is most of the people who ring us. Nobody will comment on it. We start with an examination and a plan you can spread out.

  • Do you offer finance?

    Interest free over twelve months on anything above £500, subject to a check.

  • What if I am nervous?

    Tell us when you book and we will give you a longer appointment. Sedation is available for longer treatments.

  • Do you see children?

    Yes, from the age of three. Their first visit is a look and a ride in the chair, nothing else.
